About the Project

PeregrinoBits was established with a core mission: to promote cryptocurrency literacy and champion the principles of financial self-sufficiency. In an increasingly digital world, true sovereignty still relies on the fundamental ability to hold your own keys.

By utilizing physical, non-electronic cold storage, this initiative aims to demystify self-custody. It transforms abstract digital concepts into tangible, secure assets that anyone can understand and physically hold.

Beyond the hardware, this project is designed to bridge the gap between global, virtual networks and physical, real-world communities. By carrying the Peregrino Edition by foot along the Camino de Santiago trail and giving it directly to fellow pilgrims, PeregrinoBits connects the decentralized, borderless ethos of Bitcoin with the shared endurance and human connection of the ancient pilgrimage.
