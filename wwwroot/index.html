PeregrinoBits

Tus llaves. Tu camino. An ancient path. A modern standard.

Bridging the spirit of the Camino de Santiago pilgrimage with modern financial sovereignty. A limited physical Bitcoin cold storage release, built on Ballet's proven, non-electronic physical wallet architecture — no battery, no screen, nothing to charge.

Series 1 is on sale now: 100 hand-numbered cards, available on Bitcointalk.

Two unique editions

Limited Edition — strictly limited to 100 units worldwide, each sequentially hand-numbered in archival gold metallic ink and hand-sealed in a presentation envelope with a commemorative Certificate of Authenticity and a custom wax stamp.

Peregrino Edition — never sold online. Carried on foot along the Camino and given to pilgrims met along the way, inscribed with “Ultreia” in place of a serial number.

Every card ships completely unloaded, with a zero balance, for trustless self-custody and zero regulatory friction.
